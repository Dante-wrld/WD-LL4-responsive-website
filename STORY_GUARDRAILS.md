# Story continuity: the boundaries we should not break

1. **Chaminuka is the protagonist**, a prophetic newborn from the fictional kingdom of Dzimbahwe. His abilities include communion with spiritual beings, healing, and astral projection. His power has costs; his infant body is physically vulnerable.
2. **The king orders the baby killed** because the prophecy is perceived as a threat to the throne. The king's hunters are an ongoing narrative threat, not random monster mobs.
3. **One main consciousness:** the first player represents newborn Chaminuka's projected spirit; extra players are protective ancestral echoes. They may occupy temporary vessels without creating four separate newborns. After the sixteen-year jump, each player plays their own campaign version of the older Chaminuka, with independent progress.
4. **The escape ends in Mavambo, not at the palace door.** This prototype makes the palace playable, then uses a skippable montage for the Red River and border. A complete future version should make all five opening stages playable.
5. **Sixteen years pass** between sanctuary delivery and the main playable Chaminuka. Do not introduce an adult protagonist before the time jump.
6. **Distinct build choices**, not fixed classes. Royal Warrior, Spirit Vessel, Shadow Hunter and Elemental Adept are starting specializations. As the RPG matures, weapons, stats, healing, spiritual possession and combinations should be player-driven.
7. **After the time jump, personal progress remains separate**. Multiplayer combat/invasions may interrupt another campaign but must never unlock the defender's checkpoint for the invader. The King's Bounty system, luck buffs for beating stronger opponents, and penalties for losing to weaker opponents belong in later milestones.
8. **Magic is rare**: the fictional world's design premise is 90% without innate magic, 10% with magic, while Chaminuka is exceptionally gifted. This isn't a real-world claim.
9. **Historical and spiritual respect:** this is a fictional secondary world with architectural influence from southern African stone-building traditions, not a documentary. Shona spiritual beliefs and named figures should not be represented as historically accurate game mechanics without consultation and research. The name Chaminuka has real cultural resonance; do not portray the game as a faithful retelling of actual history.
10. **Musical rights:** songs by Jah Prayzah can be discussed as creative references if supplied by the user, but game distribution requires the necessary rights. The current build uses only code-generated sound effects and optional local TTS.

## Planned chapters after the prototype

- **Chapter Zero, The Child Who Refused the King:** Royal Nursery → Broken Gate → Red River → Border of Thorns → Mavambo Sanctuary (only the palace and montage currently represented).
- **Chapter One, Sixteen Years Later:** Playable young Chaminuka, personal training, exploration of Mavambo and the first bounty rumors.
- **Further chapters:** other kingdoms, divine communication, moral dilemmas, companions, bosses, custom builds, and contract-based player invasions while keeping story checkpoints independent.
