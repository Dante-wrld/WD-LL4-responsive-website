**Description (paste into Handshake):**

Chaminuka: The Exiled Prophet is a real-time browser multiplayer fantasy adventure inspired by a fictional medieval African kingdom. Up to four friends join with a short room code, no accounts or installations required. The story begins with a newborn child condemned by a king who fears an ancient prophecy. The child survives by projecting his spirit into palace guards and servants. Players must cooperate, borrow bodies, avoid suspicion, and carry the infant through the palace gate. A journey to the sanctuary of Mavambo follows, then a sixteen-year time jump. In Act II, Chaminuka becomes a playable warrior with four selectable combat specializations, astral projection, dodge-and-spear combat and four shrine encounters. Illustrated and video cutscenes can be skipped individually, while shared story barriers keep every player's screen in sync. Persistent room recovery, records and separate palace/shrine leaderboards encourage repeat play.

**How to play (short):** Create a room and share the four-letter code. Use WASD to move, E to possess or release a person, F for their role action, and Space to pick up or set down the infant. The servant can carry the child through the servants' gate. After reaching Mavambo, fight using spear strikes, dodges and astral projection. Select your Act II build in the lobby.

**Cover image:** `submission-cover.png` in the project package.

**Technical implementation:** Node.js HTTP + ws WebSockets, server-authoritative game simulation at 30Hz, rooms for up to 4 players, video byte-range streaming, JSON checkpoint persistence with reconnect tokens, browser Canvas renderer.

**Current limitations (do not claim implemented):** no targeted PvP invasions, no open-world 3D exploration, no truly independent story campaign, no confirmed live hosted demo yet. The game is a playable top-down multiplayer vertical slice.
