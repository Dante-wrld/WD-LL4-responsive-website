THE TWO CLOCKS

Suspicion is the ring around you. It only fills while somebody can actually see you, and the HUD tells you exactly why — a soldier in the nursery, or anyone but a servant carrying a royal infant. Fill it and they realise you aren't who you look like.

The host is the bar beneath it. Every body wakes. Let it run out and you're thrown clear where you stand and he shouts. Step out on purpose with E and he slumps quietly instead — but a patrol that walks past a slumped man knows something is wrong, so choose your corner.

You cannot die. The worst that happens is you lose a body. You also can't climb back into the one you just left, so keep moving.

Unhoused you're invisible and can drift through doors to scout — but the tether pulls you back to the cradle when your spirit runs low.

The house is the third clock, across the top. It climbs when you're rumbled, when somebody finds a man you left slumped, or when the child is found out of his cradle. At each threshold more patrols walk in; fill it and they take him back and you start the night again.

Locked doors depend on who you are wearing, not on the alarm. Carry the child into the public hall as a priest, set him down with INFANT (SPACE), then leave the priest using POSSESS (E). Wear a servant, collect the child again, and head east. You have a short protected handoff window before patrols can return him.

Two gates. The servants' door is the long safe road and only a servant carries him unremarked. The postern is short but lies inside the barracks, so only a soldier reaches it — and a soldier holding an infant is damning. The captain may use either, and is watched the whole way.
